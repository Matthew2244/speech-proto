## Why the documents are licensed separately

`DESIGN.md` is the point of this project. The code exists to prove the design
is implementable and to let people hear it; the specification is what a
manufacturer would actually build from.

A specification that cannot be freely copied into an internal engineering
document, quoted in a standards discussion, or translated is a specification
nobody adopts. CC BY 4.0 removes every obstacle to that except one: **say where
it came from.**

You may copy it, adapt it, translate it, quote it at length, and build
commercial products from it. Attribution is the only condition.
